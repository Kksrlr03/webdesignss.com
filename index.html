09 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity,
before we begin.

Something else on your mind?
Ask us directly ↗

How much does a custom website cost?+

Every project is quoted based on scope, complexity and requirements. Share your goals, the pages you need and any integrations, and we can discuss a suitable approach before preparing a quote.

How long does a website take?+

The timeline depends on scope, content readiness, integrations and feedback. We agree milestones and a realistic schedule after discovery rather than promise a one-size-fits-all turnaround.

Do you work with international clients?+

Yes. We work with English-speaking businesses in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Europe, Australia and beyond. Communication and review arrangements are agreed around time zones.

Can you redesign an existing website?+

Yes. We can review the current experience, identify what should be preserved and plan a redesign around your business. Content migration and redirects are scoped where needed.

Do you provide hosting and domain connection?+

Hosting setup and domain connection can be included in the project scope. We discuss ownership, provider costs and ongoing responsibilities before launch.

Can you integrate WhatsApp and contact forms?+

Yes. We can add a direct WhatsApp link and connect enquiry forms to an agreed email or workflow. Third-party services, consent requirements and any recurring fees are discussed in advance.

Will the website work on mobile?+

Yes. Responsive, mobile-first design is part of our approach. We test layouts, navigation and core interactions across common screen sizes.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?+

Maintenance can be scoped around content updates, technical upkeep and other ongoing needs. The responsibilities, response arrangements and costs are agreed separately.

Will my website be SEO-friendly?+

We build technical foundations such as semantic content, sensible metadata, mobile layouts and crawlable pages. Search visibility also depends on content, competition and ongoing work; rankings are not guaranteed.

What do you need from me to start?+

A short introduction to your business, goals, current website if you have one, and any preferences or constraints. Existing brand assets and approved content are useful, but we can discuss what is missing during discovery.
