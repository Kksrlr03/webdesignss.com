PRIVACY / PROJECT ENQUIRIES

This notice explains how the WEB DESIGNS website handles information you choose to share.

What you provide

The project form asks for your name, business name, email, country, business type, requirements and project details. Website and budget are optional. Do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

Why it is used

Information submitted through the live form is used to review and respond to your project enquiry, clarify requirements and discuss a potential service relationship. It is not published on this website or added to a marketing list by this form.

How delivery works

When configured, the server sends your enquiry to the studio’s business inbox using Resend as an email delivery provider. The website does not store form submissions in a public database. The hosting and email providers may process technical data needed to operate and secure their services. If Cloudflare Turnstile is configured, it is used to help distinguish genuine enquiries from automated abuse.

Preview and concept sites

When delivery is not connected, the agency form clearly says so. Downloading a brief creates a text file on your own device without sending its content. The ten fictional concept-site forms validate locally only: they do not send or save what you type. Demo shopping bags exist only in memory and reset when the page is reloaded.

Retention and your choices

Enquiry emails may remain in the business inbox while needed to manage the conversation or meet applicable obligations. You can ask the studio to correct or delete your information, subject to applicable requirements, through the configured business contact channel.

Cookies and third-party links

This website does not install analytics or advertising cookies by default. WhatsApp and email links open external services whose privacy practices apply after you leave this site. An enabled anti-spam service may process browser information for security.

Privacy questions

Contact WEB DESIGNS through the business email published on the contact section. In preview mode, contact details may not yet be configured.

Last updated: 8 September 2026. The site owner should review this notice, identify the legal business operator and set a specific retention policy before public launch.
